      What do these terms mean?
      Not Started
      Approved, but implementation has not yet begun.
      Launched
      Implementation has begun.
      Complete
      Finished. Completed projects are removed two terms after completion.
      On Track
      Being carried out according to schedule.
      Delayed
      Behind schedule or experiencing delays.
      On Hold
      Put on hold; all activities have paused.
